Self-hosting

On your phone

This route works through the web version on your computer. The desktop app runs on a changing internal port and cannot be exposed through Tailscale yet; mobile access straight from the desktop app is on the wish list.

Omnissiah is a PWA: you can "install" it on your phone as an app. To reach it safely without opening anything up to the internet you use Tailscale, a private network between your own devices only. The app keeps listening on localhost of your computer; Tailscale puts a private HTTPS address in front of it.

One-time setup

  1. Install Tailscale on your computer, where Omnissiah runs, and on your phone, and log in on both with the same account: tailscale.com/download. Your devices are now on the same private network, your "tailnet".

  2. Start the web version on your computer: docker compose up -d --build, or npm run start.

  3. Put Tailscale in front of it on your computer, so it creates a private HTTPS address that forwards to your app:

    tailscale serve --bg 3000

    If you use a different APP_PORT, fill in that port. Tailscale arranges a valid TLS certificate automatically. tailscale serve status shows you the address, something like https://<computername>.<tailnet>.ts.net.

tailscale serve keeps everything inside your tailnet. Do not use tailscale funnel: that puts your app on the public internet.

Opening and installing on your phone

  1. Make sure Tailscale is on on your phone.
  2. Open the https://<computername>.<tailnet>.ts.net address in the browser.
  3. Log in with your password.
  4. Install it as an app:
    • iPhone (Safari): share button → Add to Home Screen.
    • Android (Chrome): menu (⋮) → Install app or Add to home screen.

Omnissiah now sits as an app icon on your phone and opens full screen. It works as long as your computer is on, the web version is running, and Tailscale is on on both devices.
